# Workflow 06 — The Polite Chase-Up (Three Calibrated Versions, Zero Passive Aggression)

> **Who it's for:** anyone waiting on an approval, a reply, or a deliverable from someone who outranks or outruns them.
> **Time:** 20 min of rewrites → 3 min. **Any chat LLM.**

---

## The insight nobody tells you

Chase-ups don't fail on content — they fail on **tone selection**. You need three versions calibrated in advance, and you should always send the *softest one that will work* — not the one that feels good tonight.

The professional move is a ladder you climb slowly: NUDGE → FIRM → ESCALATION. Sending FIRM on day 4 doesn't make you decisive; it makes the next 6 months awkward.

## Step 1 — State the facts, not the feeling

What you're chasing, when you sent it, what it blocks. If you're annoyed, do **not** include the annoyance — it will contaminate everything downstream.

## Step 2 — Paste this prompt

```
You are my follow-up writer. Rules:

1. Write THREE versions of a chase-up about [THING], originally
   requested on [DATE]:
   NUDGE — assumes it simply slipped their mind (true 80% of the time)
   FIRM — second chase; the deadline now has consequences; say so
   ESCALATION — respectful, factual, CC visible, zero emotion

2. Each version: 3 sentences max. First sentence = the ask + the
   original date. Last sentence makes replying effortless
   ("reply 'approved' and it's done").

3. Banned phrases: "per my last email," "just circling back,"
   "as previously discussed," "gentle reminder," "just checking in."
   These all read as hostile, whatever the dictionary says.

4. Facts only. If my input contains frustration, leave it out of all
   three versions.
```

## Step 3 — The 60-second audit: the read-aloud tone test

Read the NUDGE version aloud. **If you'd wince receiving it, it's a FIRM email wearing a costume** — regenerate.

The trap specific to this workflow: **tone bleed.** AI calibrates to the emotional register of your input. If you wrote the request while annoyed, all three versions come out sharp. The fix isn't editing the output — it's regenerating from a neutral statement of facts.

Second check: the ESCALATION version should contain nothing you wouldn't want the CC to read *as your best work*. That email gets forwarded. It always gets forwarded.

## Known failure modes

| The AI will try to… | Your fix |
|---|---|
| Absorb your frustration into "subtle" phrasing | Neutral facts in, or regenerate |
| Add wit/sarcasm it thinks is clever | Banned-phrase list + read-aloud test |
| Skip to ESCALATION energy in the FIRM version | The ladder exists to be climbed slowly |

## Why this works

Three pre-calibrated versions stop you from sending FIRM on a bad day when NUDGE was the move. The ladder gives you escalation *without* being the escalation person — and everyone knows the difference.
