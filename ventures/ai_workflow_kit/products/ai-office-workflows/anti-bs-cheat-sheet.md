# The Anti-BS Cheat Sheet

> One page. Pin it next to your monitor. This is every audit rule in the kit, condensed.

---

## The Universal Five (apply to ANY AI output at work)

**1. CAUSALITY IS THE LIE.**
AI doesn't invent content — it invents *causality*. Every sentence with
"because / due to / caused by / as a result / impacted" is a fabricated
connection until you verify it. (Workflow 01)

**2. REASSURANCE NEEDS A NUMBER.**
"On track," "smoothly," "under control" — with no number in the same
sentence, these are cover-up words. Cut them or attach evidence. (03, 09)

**3. ZERO GAPS = IT INVENTED.**
Real-world data, meetings, and weeks are full of gaps and orphans. A
summary with none means the AI "completed" reality. Missing data is
information. (02, 05)

**4. SPOT-CHECK TWO CITATIONS.**
Page refs, quotes, numbers — pick two, verify against source. If one
fails, verify everything. AI citations are wrong 10-20% of the time. (04, 05)

**5. MACHINES SORT, HUMANS COMMIT.**
Anything involving money, dates, promises, or decisions gets escalated —
never drafted. AI's optimism bias points exactly where it costs money. (10)

## Per-workflow one-liners

| # | Workflow | Its audit in one line |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Weekly update | Check every "because" — AI wires unrelated facts into fake cause-effect |
| 02 | Meeting notes | No quote, no decision; zero orphans = invented consensus |
| 03 | Status email | Reassurance word without a number = cut it |
| 04 | Document summary | Verify 2 page citations; no surprises = it skimmed |
| 05 | Spreadsheet | Recompute one number; empty GAPS = filled-in fiction |
| 06 | Chase-up | Read aloud — if you'd wince, it's FIRM in a costume |
| 07 | PPT outline | Spine alone must tell the story; two ideas = two slides |
| 08 | Resume | Impressive bullet with no number you gave = interview trap |
| 09 | Manager's mind | All questions feel easy = the AI softballed you; rerun meaner |
| 10 | Client triage | Any draft that commits (money/date/promise) goes to ESCALATE |

---

## When AI is safe vs. when it isn't

| Safe (AI drafts, you skim) | Dangerous (AI sorts, YOU decide) |
|---|---|
| Restating what you already know | Anything with a price in it |
| Formatting and structure | Anything with a deadline attached |
| Finding what you could find, faster | Anything that becomes a promise when sent |
| First drafts nobody sees | Anything a senior person will read as final |
