The thing prompt packs never mention

AI doesn't invent content. It invents causality.

You paste two separate facts — a client rework on Tuesday, a schedule slip on Friday — and the AI merges them into a clean sentence: "the slip was caused by the rework." Neither caused the other. Nobody said it did. But it reads well, so it ships — until your manager repeats it upward as fact.
